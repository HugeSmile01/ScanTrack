About ScanTrackSys

ScanTrackSys is an innovative QR code-based attendance tracking system designed for DepEd teachers. With this system, teachers can easily manage student attendance, generate QR codes, and export reports in the DepEd SF2 format.

System Features

  • QR Code Generation: Generate unique QR codes for students using their LRN and other information.
  • Attendance Tracking: Track student attendance in real-time using QR code scans.
  • Export Reports: Easily export attendance records in the DepEd SF2 format for official use.
  • Secure Login: Teacher authentication using official DepEd email accounts.

Our Mission

ScanTrackSys aims to streamline the process of tracking attendance for DepEd teachers and improve the accuracy of records while ensuring a smooth and user-friendly experience. We are committed to providing a reliable tool for teachers to manage their classrooms more efficiently.
